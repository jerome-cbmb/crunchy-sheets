────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Response (332 tokens out, 5540 tokens in, 10.6s):

Adding a new revenue stream is a structural change that touches multiple tabs. Here's how to do it cleanly:

**1. P&L Tab (rows 4-6):**
- Insert a new row after row 5 (Services Revenue)
- Label it (e.g., "Partner/Referral Revenue")
- Blue font your monthly inputs or formula
- Update Total Revenue formula to include the new row

**2. Assumptions Tab:**
- Add assumption rows for the new stream's drivers
- Example: "Partner Revenue % of MRR" (blue input)
- Add named ranges if you'll reference them often

**3. KPIs Tab:**
- Consider a new row showing the mix % of total revenue
- Formula: `=NewStream/TotalRevenue`

**4. Scenarios Tab:**
- Add rows for how this stream behaves in Bull/Bear cases

**Key decision:** Is this stream formulaic (e.g., 10% of subscription revenue) or hard-coded monthly? 
- Formulaic = cleaner, fewer inputs, easier scenarios
- Hard-coded = more flexible, more maintenance

**Watch out for:**
- Cell references in rows 7+ will shift down. Check that B7 still captures all revenue rows.
- COGS: Does the new stream have different margin economics? If so, add a corresponding COGS line.

Want me to implement a specific revenue stream? Tell me what it is and I'll wire it up.
