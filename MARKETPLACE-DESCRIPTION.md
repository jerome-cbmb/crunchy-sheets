# Crunchy Sheets — Google Workspace Marketplace Description

## Long-Form Description (4000 characters)

**For: Google Workspace Marketplace form**

---

Crunchy Sheets is an AI financial analyst that lives inside your Google Sheets sidebar. It understands your workbook the way a CFO would — formulas, cross-tab references, named ranges, structural logic — and responds like one too.

Ask it anything: "What's driving the variance in Q4?" "Build me a 13-week cash flow forecast." "Break down these numbers by customer segment." "Prove that answer with color-coded formulas." It references your actual data and builds analyses like a skilled collaborator.

Every proposed change appears in your sidebar before it touches your spreadsheet. Review, cherry-pick, or apply everything at once. You're always in control.

**The Proof tab changes everything.** Ask "Prove it" and Crunchy Sheets builds a working model that traces every number back to its source. Blue cells for revenue, red for expenses, green for derived values, gold for your answer. It's how financial analysts think — showing their work so you can trust it.

**Built by a CPA who spent years building financial models by hand.** This isn't a generic chatbot. Crunchy Sheets is a finance-native tool built with deep appreciation for Google Sheets and the teams who live in them.

Crunchy Sheets comes with 12 built-in skills: Variance Analysis, Waterfall charts, Cash Flow models, Reconciliation helpers, KPI dashboards, margin analysis, headcount planning, cohort analysis, and more. Each one uses your actual data and your sheet structure — not generic templates.

**Perfect for:**
- Controllers and CFOs managing month-end close
- FP&A teams building forecasts and dashboards
- Finance managers reconciling accounts and tracking variance
- Founders in pre-Series A/B companies running finance lean
- Any team that lives in Google Sheets and wants an AI analyst in the room

Crunchy Sheets is free during beta. No credit card required. OAuth security verified by Google.
